FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

What actually happens on the call?
Fifteen minutes with James, not a sales script. We map a show format that fits your business, sketch your first episodes and give you exact costs. If it's not a fit, we'll say so — and the plan is still yours.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the format and how often you record, so we price it on the planning call — you'll leave with exact costs, and series get bulk pricing. One session covers everything: studio, full audio and video, and a producer running the day.
I've never podcasted before. Does that matter?
Most of our clients hadn't either. If you can hold a conversation with a client, you can record an episode — the producer handles structure, pacing and everything technical.
How much of my time does it take?
That's up to you — weekly, fortnightly or monthly all work, and many clients bank several episodes in one sitting. We handle editing, clips and publishing, so your involvement ends when the recording does.
What if nobody listens?
For a service business, a podcast earns its keep well before the download numbers do. It gives prospects a reason to meet you, gives referrers something to send, and gives your socials and sales conversations a steady supply of clips.
Do you handle editing and publishing?
Yes. First cut in three to four days as standard, and we can publish straight to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube under your account — or hand you finished files to publish yourself.
